# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - 2026-10-06

First release.

- Text and image labels with shape, size, colors, corner radius, nine positions and text variables.
- Separate appearance for the product page, listings and the cart areas, each falling back to the previous one.
- Rules: categories, attribute conditions, price range, on sale with minimum discount, new products, stock status,
  store views, customer groups, active dates, priority, stop further labels, maximum labels per product.
- Manual assignment from the product edit page.
- "Use for Parent" and badges that follow the chosen variant of configurable products.
- `majistar_product_labels` indexer (Update on Save and Update by Schedule) with page and product card cache refresh.
- Hyvä storefront: product page, product cards, cart page, mini-cart and checkout summary.
- REST and SOAP API, asynchronous bulk, base64 image upload, `extension_attributes.majistar_product_labels` on products.
- Italian translation.
